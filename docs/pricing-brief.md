# AECAI Pricing Strategy Brief

**Date:** February 2026
**Prepared for:** Internal Review

---

## Value Proposition

Each page processed by AECAI replaces ~30 minutes of manual lighting fixture takeoff. At typical electrical estimator rates ($60-100/hr fully loaded), that represents **$30-50 of labor savings per page**.

A single 15-page drawing set saves a firm **$450-750 in labor** per run.

---

## Pricing Tiers

| | Standard | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Price** | $200 | $500 | Custom |
| **Pages Included** | 10 | 30 | Unlimited |
| **Effective $/Page** | $20.00 | $16.67 | Negotiated |
| **Value Captured** | ~5-6% | ~4-5% | Negotiated |
| **Customer ROI** | 15:1 | 18:1 | - |

---

## Why This Works

**For the customer:** The ROI is immediate and obvious. A firm on the Standard plan spends $200/mo to save $300-500 in labor — the tool pays for itself on the first drawing set. Professional tier customers processing 30 pages save $900-1,500/mo in labor for a $500 investment.

**For us:** We capture 4-6% of the value we create. This is low enough that price is never the objection, but high enough to build a real business. At 100 Professional customers, that is $600K ARR.

## Key Decisions Still Needed

1. **Overage pricing** — Do we charge per page beyond the cap (suggested: $25/page) or require an upgrade? Overage is better; it avoids cutting off a customer mid-project.

2. **Annual discount** — Suggested 15% discount for annual commitment ($170/mo Standard, $425/mo Professional). Locks in revenue and reduces churn.

3. **Trial period** — No free tier, but consider a 7-day trial or a single demo PDF upload so prospects can see output quality before buying.

4. **Team pricing** — Not needed at launch, but plan for it. When a firm wants 5+ seats, offer a shared page pool at a volume discount.
